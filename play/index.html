    How RP works

    A correct guess earns RP. A wrong guess, running out of time, or leaving a round loses it.

    Each division takes more RP to fill the higher you climb, and a correct guess is worth a little less while a wrong one costs a little more.

    Every round is also weighted by how rare its answer is at the moment. While most partners are bots, catching a human is worth a lot and confirming a bot only a little, so always guessing the same thing never pays off. Only reading the conversation does.
